🍽️Eat well to stay strong! Did you know? People with Parkinson’s disease and dementia often lose muscle without noticing and become increasingly weak—this is sarcopenia at work! 💪

Do not worry～This lecture, “Nutrition Guidance for People with Parkinson’s Disease and Dementia: How to Prevent Sarcopenia,” will help you easily understand what and how to eat for proper nutrition and muscle strength, helping older adults keep moving and stand steadily!

A dietitian will share practical tips so caregivers can easily learn to prepare meals wisely～Come learn a useful tip and protect your health; let us preserve older adults’ mobility, starting with food! 🥦🥚🍚
