1140819 (ROC) Building Safe, Trusting Care Relationships: The Art of Interacting with People with Dementia

The saved article summary appears below; the complete article has yet to be added.

Do interactions with people with dementia leave you frustrated or helpless? With the right approach, you can build safer, more trusting care relationships! This lecture shares communication characteristics throughout the course of dementia and teaches you to adjust language and behavior for smoother interactions. Practical examples, discussion, and useful techniques help caregivers accompany people with dementia with greater confidence and love. Let us learn this gentle, practical “art of interaction” together!

Date: 114.08.19 (ROC calendar), Tuesday. Time: 14:00–16:00. Topic: Building Safe, Trusting Care Relationships: The Art of Interacting with People with Dementia.
