1140823 (ROC) Sleep Disorders in People with Parkinson’s Disease

The saved article summary appears below; the complete article has yet to be added.

Did you know? More than 70% of people with Parkinson’s disease have trouble sleeping well or deeply! From difficulty falling asleep, waking at night, and daytime drowsiness to common rapid-eye-movement sleep disorders, these problems cause fatigue and can also affect the illness and quality of life. This lecture examines common sleep disorders in Parkinson’s disease in depth, explaining their underlying causes and possible responses. People with Parkinson’s and their families are invited to join us to find better ways to improve sleep and daily energy, so sleeping well no longer feels difficult!
