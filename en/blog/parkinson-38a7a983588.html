Solution to the recent web login issue when entering the “doctor access code”

The saved article summary appears below; the complete article has yet to be added.

If the web version fails to log in after you enter the “Doctor’s Viewing Verification Code,”

first check whether the version displayed at the bottom of the webpage is v1.07.

Some browsers load the old v1.06 from their cache, causing login failure;

go to the browser settings and look for “Privacy and Security,” “Delete Browsing Data,” “Clear Cache,” or related settings.

Then refresh the page; once v1.07 is displayed, it should work.
