First time using it: click【Register】at the bottom of the screen

@. Name: You may enter a nickname, code, English name, or anything sufficient to identify you; your full name is not required.

@. Set a password: 6-20 characters are sufficient (remember the password you set for future logins or use on different devices).

@. SMS verification code: Use a number that can receive text messages; a family member’s number is also acceptable.

Question: What if the older adult does not have a smartphone?

Answer: A family member can register and enter information using their own phone. For follow-up visits, write down the “Mobile Phone Number” and “Doctor’s Viewing Verification Code” for the patient to bring to the clinic.

Question: What if the phone screen is too small?

Answer: You can also use the registered phone number and password to log in on a tablet (enter them on an iPad).
