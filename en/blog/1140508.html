1140508 (ROC) Basic Life Support and Emergency Wound Care

The saved article summary appears below; the complete article has yet to be added.

When an unexpected accident happens, do you know what truly saves a life? According to statistics, thousands of people with out-of-hospital cardiac arrest (OHCA) face life-threatening danger every year; first aid within the crucial four minutes is their only hope of survival. This lecture, “Basic Life Support and Emergency Wound Care,” will help you learn practical CPR+AED skills, from theory to hands-on practice, so you can become a crucial lifesaver at the scene! Whether a family member collapses, someone loses consciousness on the street, or an emergency occurs in daily life, learning the correct response can save a precious life. The lecture is free, places are limited, and this is a valuable opportunity. Register now to provide greater protection for yourself and those you love!
