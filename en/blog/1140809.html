1140809 (ROC) Mental Health Conditions in Parkinson’s Disease and Their Management

The saved article summary appears below; the complete article has yet to be added.

Parkinson’s disease affects more than movement; it also involves many easily overlooked “invisible symptoms”! Did you know that depression, anxiety, insomnia, and even dementia may be related to it? This lecture explains mental health symptoms and risk factors that may appear at different stages, helping families and people with Parkinson’s recognize them early and respond effectively. Join us in caring for mental health in Parkinson’s disease!
