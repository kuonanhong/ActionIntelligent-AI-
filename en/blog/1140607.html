🎶Can dancing and singing be rehabilitation too? Yes, really! 💃🕺

Everyone is welcome to “Joyful Movement and Singing—Music Therapy in Parkinson’s Care.” Move and sing with music, and switch on the brain’s happiness! No musical expertise or dance experience is required: just come along, and we will get you moving with a smile～

Train your steps through rhythm, soothe emotions with melody, and strengthen understanding and interaction within the family! The session is enjoyable, playful, and practical, turning rehabilitation into a relaxed, fun music party🎤🎵

Invite family and friends to dance toward health and sing for a better mood!
