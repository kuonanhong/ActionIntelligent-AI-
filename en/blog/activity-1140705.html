1140705 (ROC) Nutrition Guidance for People with Parkinson’s Disease and Dementia: How to Prevent Sarcopenia

This page preserves an original activity report. Some wording about diet and medication timing needs review by the medical team; do not adjust medication based on this page.

Activity article with editorial update

Adopting a healthy lifestyle and diet can substantially reduce your risk of chronic disease.
Muscles leave if you do not train them; protein gets grumpy if you do not eat it!

Watch on YouTube: Video

🎯 **Eat well, move well, and get the right sunlight!

Prevent osteoporosis and sarcopenia**

✅ 1. Sunlight helps absorption

☀️ When drinking milk for calcium, make sure to also get sunlight to help synthesize vitamin D;
vitamin D helps the intestines absorb calcium and protects bones from loss.
👉 Ideally, expose your hands and feet to sunlight for 10~15 minutes every day.

✅ 2. Eat more antioxidant foods

🥦 Antioxidant foods reduce inflammation and protect cells:
Green peppers, colored bell peppers, guavas, and kiwifruit are all high in vitamin C!
💧 Remember to eat them fresh and avoid cooking them too long; vitamin C is easily destroyed above 60°C.
🍃 Wash them thoroughly and rinse away pesticides for greater peace of mind.

✅ 3. Consume good fats in moderation

🥜 Good fats protect cardiovascular health:
Choose more unsaturated fats (fish oil, olive oil, nuts) and fewer saturated fats (pork belly, lard).
✨ Eat a small handful of nuts every day (about the size of a thumb); avoid honey-glazed or salted nuts.

✅ 4. Eat enough protein

💪 Prevent sarcopenia:

General population: 1 gram of protein per kilogram of body weight per day

  • People with sarcopenia: 1.2 ~ 1.5 grams per kilogram of body weight per day
  • Editorial update (2026-10-09): Plan protein intake with your doctor or dietitian for your individual needs. Protein-rich meals can affect levodopa benefit in some people. Discuss meal and medication timing with your care team; do not independently reduce essential nutrition or change prescriptions.
    Sources: Parkinson’s Foundation — Diet & Nutrition · Parkinson’s Foundation — Nutrition and PD

✅ 5. How should you choose protein?

  • Animal sources (best absorbed): fish, chicken, eggs, milk, lean pork
  • Plant sources: tofu, soy milk, soybeans, nuts
  • Red meat (beef, lamb, pork) is high in fat; eat it only occasionally, and choose more white meat for better health.

✅ 6. How should you arrange your plate?

🥗 【My Plate Principles】

  • 1/2 vegetables and fruit
  • 1/4 beans, fish, eggs, and meat (protein)
  • 1/4 whole grains and other grains
  • Eat 3 servings of vegetables (fist-sized) and 2 servings of fruit (fist-sized) each day.
  • Drink 1~2 cups of milk every day to get enough calcium.

✅ 7. Helpful tips

  • ✅ Include protein in every meal
    ✅ Drink more water
    ✅ Exercise regularly
    ✅ Get sunlight
    👉 Take care of your bones, muscles, immunity, and brain together!

📌 If needed, consult a dietitian or doctor; eating well brings peace of mind!
