網站身分與內容定位

smartaction.org.tw 原站是協會公開資訊的來源之一；GitHub Pages 整理版提供可維護的知識、資源與互動入口。原站資料與本次新增編輯內容分開標示；本次新增題庫、摘要、翻譯、工具與 AI 回答，未宣稱已獲醫師逐項審閱或院方背書。

本網站提供巴金森氏症衛教與社群學習資訊。個人診斷、用藥、運動安排及症狀變化，請與自己的醫療團隊討論。

來源如何使用

醫療知識優先採用可追溯的公共衛生機構、醫療與巴金森專業組織資料，包含 WHO、NINDS、NICE 與 Parkinson’s Foundation 等。每題附對應來源及段落定位；網頁若沒有固定頁碼，提供實際章節名稱，避免編造頁數。

協會活動與站內導覽題，依據原站內容及上傳網站套件整理。原始來源可能持續修訂；連結內容的最新版本優先於本次網站快照。

100 題題庫的範圍

題庫共有 85 題巴金森相關知識及 15 題協會／站內資源導覽，題型為 80 題單選題、20 題簡單填空題。單選題採四或五個選項，填空題接受題目定義的合理同義詞。

提示先說明到哪一個主題或資源找答案；再由「查看來源」按鈕開啟具名來源。原始網址可收在按鈕內，方便閱讀，但不以假連結、隱形點擊或未經查核的時間戳導向答案。題庫是學習練習，分數不代表診斷或臨床能力認證。

既有文章的完整程度

此次盤點有 48 個既有文章頁：33 個包含可用摘要、14 個主要為標題／連結占位、1 個為影片紀錄頁。這些頁面的存在不等於取得完整原文；摘要與占位頁會明確說明來源狀態，並提供可追查的原站入口。未取得完整內容的頁面，不被當成完整讀過的專業全文。

YouTube 影片與秒數

從提供的 HTML、JS、JSON 與文章脈絡，辨識出 14 個不同 YouTube 影片 ID。這次並未取得所有影片的完整可核對字幕，也無法逐部完整觀看，因此不聲稱「全部看過」，也不編造精準答案秒數。

影片卡片保留題名、來源及查核狀態；尚未驗證的片段不加入看似精確的 ?t= 跳轉時間。未來若取得影片逐字稿並核對內容，可補上「已查核起始秒數」與相關題目。現有可追溯的來源盤點記錄在 影片來源查核紀錄。

提供的對話連結與附件

本次指定的三個 ChatGPT 參考連結未能取得可閱讀的對話全文。

因此以使用者提供的 PDF 與 ZIP 網站套件作為需求與舊版內容依據，沒有把不可讀取的連結視為已讀。資料盤點與醫療來源核對是不同工作，不以先前對話取代正式醫療參考資料。

多語版本與地區

主頁導覽介面提供 22 種語言；巴金森題庫完整內容提供繁體中文與英文，聰動小工具、消費與投資、育樂及本編輯說明也以繁中／英文為完整版本。尚未翻譯的次頁會清楚回退為英文，不把主頁介面語言數當成全站醫療文章已完成的翻譯數。

預設依瀏覽器語言與使用者選擇顯示；語言不是使用者精確地理位置的證明。網站不為了換語言暗中取得定位。重要醫療資訊請參考原始來源；翻譯不替代醫療人員的說明。

知識助理與離線 AI

知識助理提供有來源的本站知識檢索，以及需要裝置支援與模型檔案的實驗性離線文字生成。模型下載後可在本機執行的範圍，以助理頁面的能力檢查與說明為準；不保證所有手機或瀏覽器均能流暢生成。

生成式回答可能出錯，不是診斷、用藥或個人投資建議。請查看列出的原始來源；若未載入生成模型，介面應明確顯示資料檢索模式，不把預寫答案稱作模型生成。

搜尋與閱讀體驗

巴金森氏症、非營利社群、照顧者支持、運動、復健與衛教等詞彙，只在與頁面實際內容相符處自然使用。改進以清楚標題、可閱讀內容、具名來源、可用的內部連結與一致的頁面資訊為主，不堆疊隱藏關鍵字、不偽造專業背書，也不保證 Google 排名或 AI 搜尋引用。

外部連結由讀者主動開啟。育樂與工具不以強制觀看 YouTube 影片解鎖，也不在背景替使用者製造點擊或觀看紀錄。

更新與更正

若原始資料變更、連結失效，或發現題目與來源不一致，維護者應先核對原始來源，再更新題目、解說與查核日期。醫療內容的新增與修正，建議交由熟悉巴金森照護的合格醫療專業人員審閱後公開，並僅在確有審閱紀錄時標示審閱者。

Website identity and purpose

The original smartaction.org.tw website is one source of the association’s public information. This GitHub Pages edition adds maintainable knowledge, resources and interactive entry points. Original material and new editorial additions are distinguished. Newly added questions, summaries, translations, tools and AI responses are not presented as individually reviewed by physicians or endorsed by a hospital.

The site supports Parkinson’s education and community learning. Discuss individual diagnosis, medicines, exercise plans and changes in symptoms with your own healthcare team.

How sources are used

Medical information prioritizes traceable public health agencies and specialist organizations, including WHO, NINDS, NICE and the Parkinson’s Foundation. Questions cite relevant sources and sections. Web pages without fixed page numbers use actual section names; page numbers are not invented.

Association and navigation questions draw on the original site and uploaded website packages. Sources can change; the latest original source takes precedence over this snapshot.

The 100-question collection

The collection contains 85 Parkinson’s knowledge questions and 15 association or website-navigation questions: 80 single-answer multiple-choice questions and 20 short fill-in questions. Multiple-choice questions use four or five options. Fill-in questions accept the defined reasonable alternative terms.

Hints point to a topic or resource, and a named source button opens the reference. URLs may be tucked inside clearly labeled links for readability. There are no deceptive links, hidden clicks or invented video timestamps. Scores are learning feedback, not a diagnosis or a clinical qualification.

Completeness of legacy articles

The inventory includes 48 legacy article pages: 33 with usable summaries, 14 mainly title/link placeholders and one video-record page. A page’s existence does not imply access to its complete original text. Summary and placeholder pages identify their source status and provide an original-site route. Unavailable full texts are not claimed to have been read in full.

YouTube videos and timestamps

The supplied HTML, JavaScript, JSON and article context yielded 14 distinct YouTube video IDs. Complete, verifiable transcripts were not available for all videos, and every video could not be watched in full. No claim is made that all videos have been reviewed; precise answer timestamps are not invented.

Video entries retain titles, sources and verification status. Unverified passages do not receive apparently precise ?t= jumps. Verified start times and related questions can be added after transcripts and clips are checked. See the video source audit for the current inventory.

Conversation links and supplied files

The three specified ChatGPT reference links did not yield readable full conversation text.

The supplied PDFs and ZIP website packages were used to establish requirements and legacy content. Unreadable links were not treated as read. An inventory of supplied material is distinct from medical source verification; earlier conversations do not replace formal medical references.

Languages and location

The homepage interface offers 22 languages. The complete Parkinson’s quiz is available in Traditional Chinese and English; creative tools, commerce and markets, recreation, and this policy also have full Chinese/English editions. Untranslated secondary pages explicitly fall back to English. The number of homepage interface languages does not imply that all medical articles have been translated into those languages.

Initial language follows browser preferences and the visitor’s choice. Language is not proof of a visitor’s physical location. The site does not silently request location to change language. For important medical information, check the original source; translation does not replace professional explanation.

Knowledge assistant and offline AI

The assistant offers source-based retrieval from this site and experimental local text generation that needs a compatible device and model files. What can run locally after download is stated in the assistant’s capability checks and instructions. Smooth generation is not guaranteed on every phone or browser.

Generated answers can be wrong and are not diagnosis, medication instructions or personalized investment advice. Check the listed original sources. When no generative model is loaded, the interface should identify retrieval mode rather than describing prepared answers as model-generated.

Search and reading experience

Terms such as Parkinson’s disease, nonprofit community, caregiver support, exercise, rehabilitation and education are used naturally when supported by the page’s actual content. Search improvements focus on clear titles, useful writing, named sources, working internal links and consistent page information. There is no hidden keyword stuffing, invented professional endorsement or promise of Google rankings or AI search citations.

Readers choose whether to open external links. Games and tools do not require YouTube viewing to unlock and do not create clicks or views in the background.

Updates and corrections

When sources change, links fail or a question no longer matches its reference, maintainers should check the original source before updating the question, explanation and verification date. New or revised medical material should receive qualified professional review before publication; reviewers should be named only when such a review has actually occurred.
